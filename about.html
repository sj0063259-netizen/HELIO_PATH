About HELIOPATH

Smart Sustainable Infrastructure Ecosystem

HELIOPATH is a smart sustainable infrastructure project focused on integrating renewable energy, IoT automation, and intelligent campus technologies into a connected and energy-efficient environment.

The project initially began as a conceptual CAD-based solar roadway model and gradually evolved into a multi-phase infrastructure system involving smart campus planning, solar pathway integration, battery storage systems, EV charging concepts, and IoT-enabled automation technologies.

HELIOPATH combines multiple engineering domains including CAD modeling, embedded systems, IoT automation, renewable energy systems, sensor integration, and real-time monitoring dashboards.

The project currently focuses on developing smart pathway lighting systems, ESP32-based automation, sensor-controlled infrastructure, and live dashboard monitoring. Future development aims to include cloud-connected systems, AI-assisted automation, robotics integration, and intelligent energy management technologies.

HELIOPATH represents an ongoing exploration of how sustainable infrastructure and smart technologies can work together to create efficient, adaptive, and future-ready environments.
