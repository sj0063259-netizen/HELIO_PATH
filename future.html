🔬 Research & Development Tracks

Smart IoT Infrastructure

Research on sensor-based automation systems for intelligent infrastructure management and layout footprint energy optimization parameters.

Renewable Energy

Study and active optimization implementation of high efficiency photovoltaic capture nodes tailored specifically for distributed campus utilities.

Lighting Automation

Development of adaptive path safety illumination frameworks relying on sensory environmental LDR evaluation and solid state logic controllers.

Battery Systems

Research on advanced energy storage battery load balancing arrays, charging safety thresholds, and continuous local grid balancing deployment.

Smart EV Charging

Evolving micro-grid frameworks to bundle renewable-source EV charging stations layered alongside remote infrastructure allocation trackers.

Monitoring Dashboards

Development of distributed telemetry dashboards tracking energy generation vectors, system automation states, and localized sensor feeds.

Autonomous Systems

Exploration of automated robotic infrastructure verification modules and lightweight aerial tracking units for routine remote monitoring tasks.

AI-Assisted Infrastructure

Future engineering deployment of edge artificial intelligence logic models to enable predictive system maintenance routines and load balancing updates.
